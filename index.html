01 / ABOUT

The Researcher
Behind the Math

I'm a PhD Student and Research Assistant at Gebze Technical University (GTÜ), where I develop mathematical frameworks to push the boundaries of aeroelastic analysis.

My research centres on flutter analysis of flexible wing structures, leveraging state-space formulations and the Extended Galerkin Method (EGM) to model complex fluid–structure interactions with unprecedented accuracy.

I believe the future of aerospace lies at the intersection of rigorous mathematical modelling and computational power — and that's exactly where I work.
