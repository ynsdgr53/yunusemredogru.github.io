The Researcher Behind The Math

PhD Candidate &
Research Assistant

I am actively developing mathematical frameworks at Gebze Technical University (GTÜ) to push boundaries in aeroelastic structural dynamics.

My work focuses heavily on wing flutter analysis, formulating coupled aerodynamics using **state-space representations** and solving fluid-elastic behavior via the Extended Galerkin Method (EGM).

Believing that the future of next-generation green aviation belongs to the confluence of analytical methods and computation, I bridge the space between structural equations and simulation code.
